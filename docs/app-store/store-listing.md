## English

### Short description (≤ 80)

```
Okta: your school's apps, grades, attendance and alerts in one place.
```

### Full description (≤ 1000)

```
Okta is the mobile client for your educational entity on the Okta platform. Sign in and instantly reach everything your school, institute, university or training company has set up for you — all in one organized place.

Based on your role (student, guardian, teacher or administrator), Okta shows the apps and services installed for your entity and lets you open them with a tap: follow grades and marks, attendance, exams and schedules, behavior notes, notifications and announcements, and more.

Guardians can follow their children across multiple entities from a single account, and staff get quick access to the tools their administration enabled for them.

Secure by design: you only see the data and apps your role is granted. Available in Arabic and English with full right-to-left support.
```
